## Lesson -> edit

`strategy/playbook.md` gets a new dated section, "running-count pace
inputs are mechanical". A running-count projection note must state the
trailing-24h add, measured from two dated reads 24h ±3h apart. The
projection pace is that figure (or the same UTC hours one day earlier).
A sub-24h window may appear only as a labelled sanity check. A
day-over-day decay ratio needs two *measured* 24h adds and can never
come from an estimated day. Without them, use ratio 0.85 for a viral
video past day 4 (measured here ~0.9; MrBeast family similar). A note
that cites a sub-24h pace as the projection input is a rule violation on
its face. Evidence: this family, where the 4 violating rows cost +1.64
dBrier, plus the earlier MrBeast plN7JMbadRg instance (+0.35).
