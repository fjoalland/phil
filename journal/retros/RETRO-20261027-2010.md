## Strategy edit (evidence: this settlement)

Playbook: a **low-turnout poll-error rule for multi-candidate first
rounds.** The poll-average margin sd is at least 8 pts, not 5.5-6. When
pollsters' published decided-voter shares overstate the leader against the
previous round or election, shade the centre toward zero by a third of the
lead. n=1, so the margin bracket bets stay `unsourced-sd`/forecast-only
until a second event. The rule is mainly a brake: do not bet a "leader wins
by X-Y" bracket off decided-voter polls alone.
