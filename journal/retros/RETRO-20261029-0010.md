## Lesson (encoded in playbook)
Say-the-word N+ thresholds: when the per-event counts trend monotonically
in one direction over the window (3+ events), quote two Poisson rates in
the note, the window mean and the latest event alone. Est must not sit
above the higher of the two or below the lower. If the threshold lies
between the two rates' medians, est stays within 0.10 of the
latest-event rate.
