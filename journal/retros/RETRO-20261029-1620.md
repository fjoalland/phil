## Lesson -> playbook edit
- Knife-edge cap scoped: in a ladder of 3 or more buckets, the cap applies
  to the two legs' SPLIT of their combined mass, not to each leg's absolute
  probability. A leg is never raised above its distribution value by the
  cap. Evidence: 5e27ab259621 (+0.037 dBrier from the cap alone). n=1 for
  ladders; the Berlin two-way case is unchanged.
