## Lesson -> playbook

On an active, officially claimed campaign, attribution doubt is not a reason
to shade below a deep daily-strike book. Shade only when the specific day has
a named pause/truce report or credible attribution to a different force.
Evidence: the two attribution-shaded rows lost to the book (both Yes), and the
row recorded after the coalition's own claim beat it. n=3 on one market, so
this is a calibration note, not an edge class.
