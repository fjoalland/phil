## Lesson
RETRO-20261005-2015's revision rule only covers preliminary `mb`/`ml`
magnitudes. This is the first observed revision of an **`mww`** magnitude
(n=1): -0.1 within hours, on an event sitting exactly on a bracket edge
(5.9 is the bottom of 5.9-6.0). An `mww` less than ~12h old is not final
either. Playbook edit: if the deciding event is `mww` but less than 12h old
and its magnitude is exactly on a bucket edge, give at least 0.08 to the
bucket it would fall into after a 0.1 revision across that edge (instead of
~0.02-0.03), and never let it create a bet's edge. n=1, so this is
provisional. The next deep retro should sample how often fresh M5.5+ `mww`
values change in the first 24h.
