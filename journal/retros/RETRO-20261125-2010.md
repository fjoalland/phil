## Lesson / edit

The drift-decay assumption is wrong for this period. Over three weeks (Nov 4-25) I assumed a
slowdown that never showed in the series. The playbook rule now says: in AAA multi-day
touch/level models, the drift is held at the trailing 5-print mean. Any decay must be visible
in the series itself, meaning 2 consecutive prints with a smaller decline than that mean, and
must not be assumed from the calendar. Weekend prints (Sun/Mon) are excluded from that test,
because they are structurally smaller. The haircut for a plateau or reversal stays at .05-.10.
